What SoundOrder AI does

When the dining room is full and the phone won't stop ringing, orders get missed. SoundOrder AI is a voice agent that picks up every call, takes the order in natural conversation, and hands it to your kitchen the same way your staff would. Callers who want a person can always be transferred to your restaurant.

  1. Answers the call

    Picks up on the first ring, even at peak hours.

  2. Takes the order

    Understands your menu, modifiers, and a wide range of accents.

  3. Reads it back

    Confirms every item with the caller before anything is placed.

  4. Sends it through

    Pushes the order to your POS and texts the caller a summary.
