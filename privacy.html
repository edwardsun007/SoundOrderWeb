Privacy Policy

Last updated: Sep, 29, 2026

This policy explains how Smart Software Solution LLC ("SoundOrder AI", "we") collects and uses information when you call a restaurant that uses SoundOrder AI, receive text messages from us, or contact us.

Information we collect

When you call a participating restaurant, we collect your phone number, the details of your order, and a recording and transcript of the call. If you contact us directly, we collect the information you send, such as your name and email address.

How we use it

We use this information to take and place your order with the restaurant, send you an order summary, payment link, and order status updates by text message, transfer your call to restaurant staff when you ask, and improve the accuracy of our service. Call recordings and transcripts are automatically deleted after 30 days.

Text messaging

We send text messages only about orders you place by phone. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information may be shared with service providers who help us deliver messages and process orders, such as our telephony and payment providers.

Sharing

We share your order details with the restaurant you called and with the service providers needed to complete it. We do not sell personal information.

Security

Recordings and transcripts are available only to the restaurant you called and to authorized SoundOrder AI staff for support, and all staff access is logged.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. To ask about or delete your data, email edwardsun688@gmail.com.
